Capture

What is saved for each vehicle, and when.

One image is saved per vehicle. Full frame is best for training a detector.
Best frame keeps the best view of each vehicle in a buffer and replaces it whenever a better frame arrives (the older one is dropped); it is saved once, when the vehicle leaves the ROI. First frame saves instantly: faster, but a lower-quality view and more duplicates.
Off keeps a clean image-only training set. On writes a .json next to each image (class, confidence, box, time, model).
